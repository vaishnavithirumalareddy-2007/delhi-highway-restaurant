About us

A vegetarian table, set for the whole family

Delhi Highway Restaurant is a vegetarian dining destination on the road at Valluru, close to Valluramma Temple in Ongole. We built it as a place to pause, whether you are travelling through or planning a meal out with the family.

The menu runs from Delhi chaat and stuffed parathas to tandoori starters, deep-fried kebabs and Chinese favourites, with a long list of soups, shorbas and cool drinks to go with them.

Come hungry, bring everyone, and expect a warm welcome at the table.
